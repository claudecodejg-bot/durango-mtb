Local Trail Tips

Essential knowledge for riding in Durango.

🌧️

Don't Ride When Wet

Durango's clay-rich soil is extremely fragile when wet. Riding on muddy trails causes lasting damage to the tread. Check trail conditions before heading out.

🛡️

Get a CORSAR Card

The Colorado Outdoor Recreation Search and Rescue (CORSAR) card helps fund rescue operations. A small purchase that could save your life in the backcountry.

🦌

Seasonal Closures

Animas Mountain and Dalla Mountain Park close seasonally for wildlife protection. Check for additional falcon nesting closures before riding.

⇆

Two-Way Traffic

Most Durango trails are multi-directional. Ride in control, call out when passing, and yield to uphill riders and hikers.

⚡

Start Early at Altitude

High-country trails like Engineer and Colorado Trail sections are best ridden in the morning. Afternoon thunderstorms are common above treeline.
